Definition 1.3.26. Function Composition.

Let \(g:A \to B\) and let \(f:B \to C\text{.}\) The composition of \(f\) and \(g\), denoted by \(f\circ g : A \to C\) is defined, for all \(a \in A\text{,}\) by \((f \circ g)(a) = f(g(a))\text{.}\)
